Before you take a Gulf job, check the offer.

Looking for work abroad can bring opportunity, but a convicing advert is not proof that a recruiter is licensed. Check the agency, ask for written terms, and know where to seek help before paying or travelling.

Start with three checks.

Check the recruiter
Independently confirm the current licence through the appropriate official Ugandan register.
Read the terms
Ask for a written contact showing the employer, job, destination, pay, hours, and accommodation.
Know where to turn
Keep copies of documents and helpful contacts before travelling.
